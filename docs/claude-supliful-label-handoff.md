# Supliful Label Rebuild Handoff

## Goal

Create a **new, separate test label** for Supliful's `Creatine Hydration Powder` product. Do not alter any existing labels or product drafts.

The objective is a clean female-facing visual variant that can later be applied to other products. It must look intentional in the Supliful bottle mockup, not merely be a recolored template.

## Product Facts: Use Exactly These

- Product: Creatine Hydration Powder
- Flavor: Lemon
- Serving: 10 g scoop
- Creatine monohydrate: 5,000 mg / 5 g
- Sodium: 1,000 mg / 1 g
- Potassium: 200 mg
- Magnesium: 60 mg
- Product amount: 300 g / 10.6 oz
- Verified attributes: gluten-free, lactose-free, allergen-free, antibiotic-free, hormone-free, alcohol-free, halal

Do not add an unsupported claim such as sugar-free, vegan, GMO-free, hormone-balancing, or a clinical/performance promise.

## Format

- Supliful built-in label trim size: `11 in W x 3 in H`.
- For a flat external upload, Supliful requires `11.25 in W x 3.25 in H` including bleed, RGB, max 10 MB, PDF/PNG/JPG, ideally 600 DPI.
- The label must retain clear top space for the Bluom logo and clear lower space for Supliful's required Dietary Supplement / net weight / facts / caution sections.
- The final physical label needs Bluom's business name and legal address before it is published. Do not publish without those details.

## The Failed Attempt: Do Not Repeat

The previous AI edit used five very small round icon cards. It failed because:

- The numeric values overflowed their circles.
- Labels broke into individual letters and extended below the card row.
- Numbers and labels overlapped each other.
- The five-card row was too small to carry `200MG`, `60MG`, `POTASSIUM`, and `MAGNESIUM` legibly.

The result is unusable. Do not try to fix this by shrinking or stacking those labels further.

## Recommended Design Direction

Use a feminine but athletic system: not pink-for-pink's-sake, and not a different product formula.

1. Work on a copy only, named `BLUOM Creatine Hydration - Women's Test`.
2. Keep the hierarchy extremely simple:
   - `BLUOM` logo at the top.
   - `CREATINE` as the primary product name.
   - `HYDRATION` as a smaller subtitle.
   - A restrained gradient or solid background with strong contrast.
3. Use **three large factual widgets max**, not five small circles:
   - `5G CREATINE`
   - `1G SODIUM`
   - `10G SERVING`
4. Place `200MG POTASSIUM` and `60MG MAGNESIUM` in a single compact, readable line below the three widgets, or omit them from the marketing panel. They remain on the Supplement Facts panel.
5. Prefer rounded rectangles, pill facts, or a clean horizontal facts row over the old circle-plus-half-circle workaround.
6. Do not use decorative pictogram icons unless they are part of a consistent, professionally aligned icon family.
7. Use only one display font for headings and one legible sans-serif for facts. Facts must remain readable at actual bottle scale.
8. Avoid medical claims and avoid implying that this has a formula unique to women. It is a women-facing visual edition of the same formula.

## Mockup QA Checklist

Before saving, view the label in Supliful's mockup and verify:

- Every word remains inside its widget or text container.
- No wrapping creates single-letter columns.
- Facts can be read without zooming dramatically.
- The logo, product name, facts row, and regulatory area have clear separation.
- Background contrast makes all text readable.
- Label artwork does not cover or crowd the Supliful-required elements.
- It looks good on a 300 g powder tub, not just on the Canva canvas.
